# The Green Winter

**An X-Files fanfic mystery.** A single-file parser adventure, playable in any browser.

> Bellwether, Oregon. Pop. 1,200. The extension agent walked into a soybean field and came back
> fine. Everyone in town is fine. The road east is closing. Scully is four hours
> out and her last text says DO NOT TOUCH ANYTHING.

## Play

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://taweret23.github.io/the-green-winter/)**

Or download `index.html` and open it. No install, no build step, no server, no
dependencies — one file, works offline.

## Commands

```
LOOK [thing]              GO [north/south/east/west/up/down]
DRIVE [place]             TAKE [item]
USE [item] ON [thing]     TALK [person]
CALL SCULLY               WAIT
INVENTORY   MAP   NOTES   HINT   HELP   RESTART
```

Side panel does most of it by clicking, if typing isn't your thing.

## Notes

- Four endings. One of them is the good one. It is not the one with the fire.
- Watch the **Rooted** meter. It goes up when the night is being nice to you.
- Keep your seeds. Keep track of how many. Both of those sentences matter, and
  they are in tension with each other, and that's the game.
- `HINT` escalates gently and never just hands you the answer.

## Content

Body horror by way of agriculture. Loss of self. Nothing graphic — the scary
part is how pleasant everyone is about it.
